# Skin Groove homepage layout prototype

Question: Which content structure best explains Skin Groove, earns trust in
virtual care, and creates a natural path to booking or retail?

This is a throwaway prototype. The current homepage remains the production
source of truth until the user selects a direction.

## Surface concept seed

- Scope: surface
- Mode: persuade
- Seed key: `efef2f6e`
- Reproduction:
  `concept-seed.mjs --scope surface --mode persuade --from efef2f6e --candidate-count 7`
- Dealt indices: 7, 3, 5

The grounded candidate list used for the roll was:

1. The Virtual Skin Room: an appointment-led welcome followed by a compact care
   map.
2. The Grooved Guidebook: educational chapters that move from skin questions to
   routine decisions.
3. The Groove Cabinet: a retail-led composition where consultation and shop are
   equal entry points.
4. The Routine Playlist: products and habits arranged as a flexible sequence.
5. The Skin Edit: an editorial manifesto followed by an asymmetric service index
   and visual collage.
6. Skin Schoolhouse: a practical learning path organized around common routine
   questions.
7. The Consultation: a consultation-first narrative that explains virtual care
   before retail.

The three dealt structures correspond to the `consult`, `cabinet`, and
`editorial` variants.

## Direction contract

### THESIS

Skin Groove turns skincare overwhelm into a clear virtual conversation, a
routine that fits real life, and a focused online product edit.

### OWN-WORLD

The established Skin Groove identity remains fixed: apricot ground, deep brown
type, burnt orange action, avocado accents, warm cream, soft curved geometry,
and the approved Righteous, DM Serif Display, and Manrope font stack.

### STORY

Each option follows a different persuasive order while preserving the same
product truth:

- The Consultation moves from virtual guidance to process, routine, product
  edit, and care philosophy.
- The Skin Edit moves from emotional relief to brand point of view, services,
  retail, and a direct invitation.
- The Groove Cabinet moves from the combined offer to retail principles, two
  entry paths, human outcome, and brand position.

### FIRST VIEWPORT

Every option must identify Skin Groove as virtual esthetics, explain the value
in plain language, show real skincare imagery, and present disabled booking and
shop placeholders without implying those systems are live.

### FORM

The Consultation uses an asymmetric photo split and a long-form service
explanation. The Skin Edit uses editorial scale, image overlap, and a
magazine-like reading rhythm. The Groove Cabinet uses a product mosaic and two
large shelf-like entry surfaces. All three collapse to a deliberate
single-column mobile layout and support system light and dark modes.

## Quality bar

- The three layouts must differ in structure, information hierarchy, and primary
  path, not only in styling.
- Visitors should understand the virtual-only model within the first viewport.
- No testimonials, clinical results, founder biography, pricing, or product
  claims may be invented.
- All commercial controls remain visibly disabled and labeled `Coming soon`.
- The original `/` route must remain unchanged.
- Body and control contrast must meet WCAG AA, keyboard navigation must work,
  and reduced motion must preserve all content.
- Display type stays at or below 6rem with tracking no tighter than -0.04em.
